# Known evidence: L11-Matched transfer from SIMPLER to LIBERO

This is the evidence ledger for the transfer diagnosis. It distinguishes measured results from hypotheses and records known data-quality limits before any additional experiment is selected.

## Current best answer

**Status: INCONCLUSIVE on the causal bottleneck.** The frozen L11-Matched recipe has clear historical SIMPLER gains but no stable mean gain in large LIBERO evaluations. The trace-faithful OSMesa held-out cohort supports an episode-level action-guidance association (Rescue-higher G AUC .914, 95% CI [.657, 1.000], n=7/5), while an available SIMPLER cohort points in the opposite direction (AUC .332, CI [.219, .453]). A preregistered closed-loop positive-G gate pilot completed neutral (41/50 vs 41/50; 4 wins / 4 losses), so the action-value association does not validate that gate. No cross-benchmark causal bottleneck is established; checkpoint, renderer, simulator, task, and state distributions remain confounded.

The SIMPLER and LIBERO results are not a pure renderer/benchmark contrast. The LIBERO evaluations use LIBERO-finetuned VQ-VLA checkpoints, while the SIMPLER study uses the OpenVLA checkpoint/configuration associated with the historical SIMPLER experiment. Benchmark, task distribution, simulator, and policy checkpoint therefore change together.

## Reliability hypothesis: corrected interpretation and replication

The cross-renderer Full RT vs Depth0 analysis is a large visual-domain shift and is **not** a test of mild nuisance stability. Its zero-heavy discrete action-delta metrics do not falsify a reliability hypothesis.

The replicated mild-view analysis is the relevant test: each state uses a single live replay RGB with identity and six small photometric variants, and continuous guidance effects on a fixed clean winner/runner-up margin. For LIBERO, the primary sign-consistency score has AUC .451 (95% episode bootstrap CI [.313, .593]); late-state AUC is .420. Its within-task macro AUC is .495 (CI [.313, .663]). A stricter all-six-view score is weakly positive but inconclusive (overall AUC .557, CI [.414, .699]; task macro .615, CI [.413, .814]). The SIMPLER pilot's AUC .700 (5 Rescue, 12 Harm; CI [.333, 1.000]) is too small to establish a reliable cross-benchmark difference. Current evidence does not support a reliability gate and does not establish that reliability is useless. Replay initial states and final labels match history 66/66, but the current selector is historically exact at only 735/3,913 sampled states (both `m` and selected IDs; `m` alone matches 1,133/3,913). See [MILD_VIEW_TRANSFER_REPORT.md](/home/leju-suzhou/zjt_ws/token-cd/artifacts/libero_transfer_diagnostics/MILD_VIEW_TRANSFER_REPORT.md).

## Negative branch probe v4: feature-kNN replacement

Completed 53/53 assigned replay cases. At least one state with exact historical `m` and selected IDs was available in 46 episodes (21 Rescue / 25 Harm; 353 trace-valid states). Every case matched its initial-state hash and historical replay outcome. No tested fresh-render RGB hash matched the historical RGB hash, so these are same-physical-state/same-current-frame paired negative-arm comparisons, not pixel-identical historical replay.

Holding current RGB, clean logits, L11 selector, `m`, selected IDs, and lambda=.5 fixed, replacing each selected token with the mean of its four nearest unselected projector features by cosine similarity changed some negative logits (mean cosine .986 Rescue/.994 Harm; negative-logit argmax changed in 12.0% vs 4.1% of action-dimension/state comparisons), but barely changed the clean top-two guided margin: episode mean change +.0047 in Rescue and −.0016 in Harm; Rescue-vs-Harm AUC .518 (95% CI [.347,.690]), task-macro .622. Harmonic and kNN guidance-margin AUCs on this replay subset were .560 and .566. Thus this local feature-kNN negative has no demonstrated action-margin benefit for Harm and gives no basis for a closed-loop pilot. It does not show that negative construction is irrelevant: only one constrained alternative was evaluated, the exact-trace subset is selected, and current RGB differs from historical RGB.

Historical mild-view logits on the same 46 episodes give harmonic guidance-margin AUC .693, versus .560 on the current fresh re-rendered frames. This contrast is descriptive and highlights the RGB replay mismatch; it is not a same-image paired causal comparison across historical and current runs. Full case/state results: [NEGATIVE_VARIANT_PROBE_V4_REPORT.md](/home/leju-suzhou/zjt_ws/token-cd/artifacts/libero_transfer_diagnostics/negative_variant_probe_v4/NEGATIVE_VARIANT_PROBE_V4_REPORT.md); machine-readable data: [NEGATIVE_VARIANT_PROBE_V4_ANALYSIS.json](/home/leju-suzhou/zjt_ws/token-cd/artifacts/libero_transfer_diagnostics/negative_variant_probe_v4/NEGATIVE_VARIANT_PROBE_V4_ANALYSIS.json).

## Spatially matched low-L11 placebo preflight (2026-09-25–26)

Using 40 existing canonical OSMesa episodes (five tasks, eight init states/task) and ten evenly spaced trace states per episode, we screened a fixed-count low-L11 mask matched to four radial bins. It achieved exact `m_t`, zero token overlap, a large L11-score separation (median mean rank percentile .924 canonical vs .202 placebo), and median normalized radial Wasserstein distance .040. However, quadrant-count mismatch was substantial (median L1 38, about 19 token assignments at median m=40), and 4-neighbor component counts matched in only 36/400 states (median 5 canonical vs 8 placebo). Existing trace artifacts do not include aligned RGB or instance segmentation, so source/target object coverage was not measured. **Preflight decision: do not launch closed-loop episodes with this candidate.** The mask construction must be revised and re-screened for quadrant/topology and target-object coverage before it can serve as a semantic placebo. Details and reproduction: [SPATIAL_PLACEBO_SANITY_REPORT.md](/home/leju-suzhou/zjt_ws/token-cd/artifacts/libero_spatial_semantic_placebo_sanity_v1_20260925/SPATIAL_PLACEBO_SANITY_REPORT.md), [analysis script](/home/leju-suzhou/zjt_ws/token-cd/artifacts/libero_spatial_semantic_placebo_sanity_v1_20260925/analyze_sanity.py), [per-state rows](/home/leju-suzhou/zjt_ws/token-cd/artifacts/libero_spatial_semantic_placebo_sanity_v1_20260925/sanity.json).

It had exact count and zero overlap in 400/400, positive attention-score gap in 400/400, median continuous radial Wasserstein .035, and 4-neighbor component difference <=1 in 383/400. ## Position-prior / debiased-L11 offline diagnostic (2026-09-26)

Estimated frozen global and task-specific 16x16 mean-attention priors from 400 sampled states in the existing OSMesa init-8..15 cohort, then evaluated them on 400 existing EGL init-0..7 states from the same five tasks. The EGL cohort is not globally untouched (used in prior offline analyses); no success labels were read and no episodes were run. Median raw Top-m overlap with deterministic position-only Top-m was 14/39 under the global prior and 18/39 under the task-specific prior. Raw Top-m retained median overlap 31/39 and 27/39, respectively, with Top-m after subtracting the frozen prior. Thus the simple prior explains a meaningful portion of spatial selection, while this additive correction leaves substantial raw-L11 structure. The result is about ranking geometry only; it does not establish semantic correctness or behavioral value. Prior-weighted spatial-null mask overlap with raw Top-m averaged 9.2/39 globally and 12.4/39 task-specifically. No closed-loop decision has been made from this diagnostic. Details, per-state rows, priors, and maps: [POSITION_PRIOR_REPORT.md](/home/leju-suzhou/zjt_ws/token-cd/artifacts/libero_position_prior_offline_v1_20260926/POSITION_PRIOR_REPORT.md), [maps](/home/leju-suzhou/zjt_ws/token-cd/artifacts/libero_position_prior_offline_v1_20260926/POSITION_PRIOR_MAPS.png), [analysis script](/home/leju-suzhou/zjt_ws/token-cd/research/semantic_token_cd/analyze_position_prior_l11.py).
