Figure X. Component ablations and intervention-budget comparison. (a) Success-rate drops relative to the full method (48.5%) on 400 seed-matched episodes; bars are grouped by ablated component, with pastel colours distinguishing variants within each group. Values are reproduced from the supplied ablation table; error bars are not shown because corresponding uncertainty estimates are unavailable. (b) Fixed-budget sweep over 16–64 tokens on four Google tasks (seeds 100–199, lambda=0.5; 400 episodes per method). Horizontal lines show Matched and the best observed TopP variant (p=0.85, clipped). Matched achieves the highest observed success rate in this sweep. The two panels use separate evaluation settings and reference success rates. Panel (b) reports descriptive point estimates, not significant superiority.
