Intervention budget sweep on four Google tasks (seeds 100–199, lambda=0.5; 400 episodes per method). Fixed budgets range from 16 to 64 tokens; horizontal lines denote Matched and the best observed TopP variant in this sweep (p=0.85, clipped). Matched achieves the highest observed success rate (213/400, 53.25%), versus 207/400 (51.75%) for the best observed fixed budget and 206/400 (51.50%) for the best observed TopP variant. Values are descriptive point estimates, not evidence of significant superiority. These results have a different evaluation setting/reference from the 48.5% full-method component ablation.
